ABOUT AUSTIN TYLER HEATH

Artist first.
Filmmaker always.

I create 3D imagery with the mindset of a filmmaker — using design, lighting, materials, and composition to make a world feel lived-in before a character ever enters the frame.

My work sits between environment art and visual storytelling, with a particular interest in stylized worlds that retain believable form, texture, and atmosphere. I enjoy taking an idea from a blank scene to an image that feels like it belongs on screen.
